Privacy Policy

Harbor Heir: Tea to Tycoon · Last updated: 30 October 2026

Harbor Heir: Tea to Tycoon (the "Game") is a mobile game made by Red Pen Studio ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information is used when you play the Game and what choices you have.

1. Summary

  • The Game works offline. You can play without an internet connection.
  • There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number to play.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device. We do not run a game server and do not receive your progress.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect some device information, described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details.

2. Game progress stored on your device

Your progress, settings and purchase status are saved in the Game's private storage on your device. This data does not leave your device through us. If Android Auto Backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup with Google, under Google's own terms; we cannot access that backup.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google, to show ads. Rewarded ads are always optional. When an ad is requested or shown, AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • device information, such as device model, operating system version, language, and approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • app-interaction data, such as which ads were shown, clicks, and how the app is used in relation to ads.

Google uses this data to deliver, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. Ads may be personalized (based on your interests) or non-personalized, depending on your choices and the law in your region. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Game shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are used. You can review or change your choice at any time from the Game's Settings screen.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Learn more about how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment, and we never see or store your card number, bank details or other payment information. We only receive confirmation from Google Play of which item was bought, so the Game can unlock it and restore it later.

5. Notifications

The Game can send optional local reminders (for example, that your businesses earned coins). These are created on your device and are not sent from a server. They are off unless you agree, and you can turn them off at any time in the Game's Settings or in your Android settings.

6. Audience

The Game is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the Game, please contact us and we will help.

7. How to delete your data

  • Reset in the Game: open Settings and use "Reset game" to erase your progress on the device.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the Game deletes all its data from your device.
  • Backups: to remove a copy saved in your Google device backup, manage your backups in your Google account or Android settings.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and manage your ad choices with Google.

Purchases made through Google Play stay linked to your Google account so they can be restored.

8. Security

Because your progress stays on your device and we run no server, we do not hold a database of player information. Data shared with Google is handled under Google's security practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's update notes.
